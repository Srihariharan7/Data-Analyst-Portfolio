Dataset information

HR Employee Dataset Overview

This project uses an employee-level dataset containing 100 records and 12 columns. It includes information about departments, job roles, locations, employment status, salary, performance, engagement, satisfaction and training.

Dataset Structure

  • Total Records: 100
  • Total Columns: 12
  • Departments: 8
  • Locations: 8
  • Worksheet: Raw_Data

Key Areas of Analysis

  • Employee distribution by department and location
  • Employment status analysis
  • Annual salary analysis
  • Performance rating analysis
  • Employee engagement and satisfaction
  • Training hours analysis

Tools Used

Excel, MySQL, Power BI, Tableau and PowerPoint.

Project Objective

To explore employee-related information, identify workforce patterns and communicate HR insights through dashboards and reports.
